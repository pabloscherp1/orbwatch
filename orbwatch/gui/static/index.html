Decay faster than the days before, spread over several updates: extra drag, usually a geomagnetic storm heating the upper atmosphere. The G4 storm of 19 January 2026 shows on every low orbit at once. Counted as drag in the budget.

SET ASIDE NOT COUNTED

Below floor: both smaller and less significant than the worst fit noise seen on satellites that cannot manoeuvre; an event at 30σ in plane or 100σ out of plane is kept whatever its size. Incoherent: steps that cancel, a bad fit rather than a burn. Excursions: a jump undone within 10 days. Tick SET ASIDE above the charts to see them.
